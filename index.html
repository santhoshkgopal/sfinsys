About Sfinsys

All-in-One Mobile Finance & Billing PWA

About Sfinsys

Sfinsys is an all-in-one Progressive Web App (PWA) designed for Bullion Market tracking, Cash Ledger management, GST Invoicing & Quotations, Jewellery Estimation, and Personal Finance. Built with a mobile-first, high-contrast interface and offline-friendly local storage with cross-device backup & sync support, Sfinsys helps shop owners, jewelers, lenders, and individuals keep their daily financial operations organized in one place.

What Sfinsys Helps You Do

Master & Party Lending Ledger

Built for legal financiers as well as everyday lenders, this module tracks credit given, principal received, interest paid, and round-off adjustments across individual party accounts or a combined master view, complete with customizable interest calculation methods (zero interest, ₹ per ₹100/month, simple % p.a., compound % p.a., and compound % p.m.), QR payment cards, CSV export, and printable/previewable statements.

Daily Gold Rates & Jewellery Estimator

Keep track of 24K (999), 22K (916), and 18K (750) gold rates per gram and calculate instant jewellery estimates using gold weight, stone weight or stone price, making charges, discounts, and GST—with one-tap WhatsApp sharing and print/preview slips.

GST Invoices & Customer Quotations

Tailored for small shop owners to create tax invoices (CGST/SGST and IGST) and customer quotations with saved seller profiles, party auto-fill, Excel/CSV item catalog import, bidirectional base-rate and inclusive-rate calculation, multiple layout and color themes, and print support for A4, A5, and 80mm thermal receipts.

Cash Vault & Passbook

Monitor cash on hand with cash-in and cash-out entries, date-range and type filters, live vault balance tracking, and printable cash movement statements.

Personal Income & Expenditure

Manage personal cash flow—including salary, additional revenue, loans and EMIs, home construction stages, and household maintenance—with budget progress tracking, CSV export, and printable summaries.

PIN Security & Backup

Protect access with a 4-digit PIN login/registration screen, profile customization, and backup/sync tools so your records stay accessible and under your control.
